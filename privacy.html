Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

Plusso is a personal budgeting app. In short: your financial data stays on your iPhone. We have no servers that store your data, there is no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Accounts, transactions, categories, notes, tags, budgets, goals, loans, trips, receipt photos and settings are saved only in the app's storage on your iPhone. We cannot see or access them.

Backups

If you turn on iCloud backup, a copy of your data is saved in your own iCloud Drive, under your Apple account and protected by Apple. You can also export a backup file manually; you decide where it goes.

Camera and photos

Plusso uses the camera or your photo library only when you attach or scan a receipt. Text on a receipt is recognized on your device with Apple's Vision technology; the image is never uploaded.

Bank statement import

CSV files you import are read on your device and are not sent anywhere.

Face ID

App lock uses Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode through iOS. Plusso only receives a "confirmed / not confirmed" answer and never has access to biometric data.

Notifications

Reminders (payday, payments, loans, summaries) are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use push servers.

Internet connections

To convert currencies, the app downloads public exchange rates from the source you choose: National Bank of Moldova, National Bank of Romania, Central Bank of Russia, National Bank of Ukraine, European Central Bank or ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). These requests contain no personal or financial data; like any website, the provider can see your IP address.

Purchases

Payments for Plusso Pro are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. Subscriptions are managed with RevenueCat, which receives a random anonymous identifier, your purchase history and basic device information (such as app version and country) to confirm your access. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Data & backup → Delete all data, or delete the app. An iCloud backup can be removed in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Children

Plusso is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Importación de extractos bancarios
